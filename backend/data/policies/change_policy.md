# Change Policy

## Flight Changes
Passengers may change their flights depending on their fare class rules. Changes may be subject to fare differences.
- **BASIC_ECONOMY**: Flight changes are not permitted.
- **FLEXIBLE**: Flight changes are permitted with no change fee, but fare difference applies.
- **PREMIUM_FIRST**: Flight changes are permitted with no change fee, and priority rebooking.

## Airline Disruption Waiver
In the event of an airline-initiated disruption (e.g., flight delay or schedule shift) of 180 minutes or more, an automatic full-refund waiver applies to all tickets, overriding the standard fare class rules. Passengers will be eligible for a 100% refund regardless of their original fare class (including BASIC_ECONOMY).
