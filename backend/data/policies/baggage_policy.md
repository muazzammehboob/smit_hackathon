# Baggage Policy

## Baggage Allowance Schedule per Class

### Basic Economy (BASIC_ECONOMY)
- **Carry-on**: 1 personal item only (must fit under the seat in front of you). No overhead bin access.
- **Checked Baggage**: 0 free checked bags. Additional bags may be purchased at standard rates.

### Flexible (FLEXIBLE)
- **Carry-on**: 1 personal item and 1 standard carry-on bag (overhead bin access included).
- **Checked Baggage**: 1 free checked bag up to 50 lbs (23 kg).

### Premium First (PREMIUM_FIRST)
- **Carry-on**: 1 personal item and 1 standard carry-on bag with priority boarding.
- **Checked Baggage**: 2 free checked bags up to 70 lbs (32 kg) each, with priority baggage handling.
